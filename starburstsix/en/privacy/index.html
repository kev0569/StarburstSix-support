Privacy policy

Draft revised: 2026-11-21

Scope & current version

This policy explains how Starburst Six and this support website handle information.

How do the free and full editions differ?

Trial games and scores are not saved or backed up. Preferences are still kept on the device.

Local data and optional iCloud

Saves, scores, optional player names, settings and gameplay statistics are stored locally. Starting with 1.5.1, optional iCloud backup, off by default, sends high scores, optional names and both difficulty saves to your Apple iCloud account, along with a backup date and random per-device identifier. Settings and aggregate gameplay statistics are not included. The developer has no server that receives these backups. No advertising or tracking SDK is included. Do not enter sensitive information as a player name.

Retention, deletion & backups

Restoring a backup requires confirmation, replaces both difficulty saves and merges the top 12 scores per difficulty. A local pre-restore copy is kept. iCloud delivery is asynchronous; an enabled switch is not proof of a completed server upload. Turning backup off or resetting local data does not remove existing iCloud backups. An Apple Account change turns backup off until you enable it again. Offline and iCloud errors do not remove local saves. Delete all Starburst Six iCloud backups for this Apple Account? This turns off backup and keeps the scores and saves on your device. iCloud may take time to apply the deletion.

Open Settings → Reset Data and confirm. This clears saved games, scores, optional names, gameplay statistics and settings. This action cannot be undone. The app cannot manage device backups.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. This website adds no analytics, advertising, cookies or browser storage and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. External links are governed by the destination provider’s policy.

Privacy questions & support messages

The developer cannot access your on-device game records. If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are used to respond and handle your request. Outlook (Microsoft) handles email under Microsoft’s privacy policy. To request deletion of support correspondence, email us. Do not send sensitive information.
