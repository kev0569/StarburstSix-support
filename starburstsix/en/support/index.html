Support

Answers for the current iPhone and iPad game.

How do the free and full editions differ?

Free download; one-time purchase unlocks the full game. Play Casual or Challenge with all boards and piece styles. Each free game ends after 100 legal moves, or earlier if no move remains. Trial games and scores aren’t saved; preferences are kept. Unlock unlimited turns, separate saved games for both difficulties, high scores and optional iCloud backup.

Where is my game saved?

In the full game: Games are saved on this device. In Settings, you can turn on iCloud backup for high scores and both difficulty saves. Restoring requires confirmation: it replaces both saves and merges up to 12 scores per difficulty. iCloud changes may take time.

Why is there no sound?

Check Sound in Settings, your media volume, silent mode and audio output. If sound does not return after switching apps, turn Sound off and on or reopen the app.

Can I change difficulty mid-game?

In the full game: Each difficulty keeps its own game. Switching saves the current game and resumes the selected difficulty; if it has no save, a new game starts. The New Game icon beside Undo restarts only the selected difficulty after confirmation. The other difficulty stays intact.

What happens when a game ends?

In the full game: The final board, score and round remain visible. Restart records the result and starts a new game on the same screen. Finish records the result and returns to the home screen. A record name is optional; no keyboard opens automatically.

How do I delete local data?

Open Settings → Reset Data and confirm. This clears saved games, scores, optional names, gameplay statistics and settings. This action cannot be undone. The app cannot manage device backups.

How do I delete iCloud backups?

In Settings, choose Delete iCloud backups and confirm. This turns off backup without changing data on your device. Turning backup off on its own does not delete existing copies.
