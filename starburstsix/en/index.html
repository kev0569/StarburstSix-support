Simple moves. Thoughtful choices.

01

Find a path

Choose a piece, then an empty cell it can reach through unoccupied cells.

02

Connect six

Six or more adjacent pieces of the same color clear together. A scoring move lets you act again without the forecast pieces arriving.

03

Leave room for the next move

If your move does not score, the forecast pieces arrive. Watch the preview and keep routes open; the game ends when no legal move remains.
